The Wizard’s Library 📚

Free practice skies for every wizard - real data from the wizard’s own telescopes, stacked and ready to process. Cloudy night? No telescope yet? The Library will lend you the stars.

Under construction

The shelves are being carved and the first data sets are being chosen from the wizard’s archive - years of imaging, from smart scopes to big rigs. Watch this page (and the videos) - the doors open soon.

What the shelves will hold

🌟 First Light

Bright, friendly targets that reward every step - perfect for your very first process, or for following along with a tutorial on the exact same data.

🌈 Dual-Band Skies

OSC dual-band stacks from smart scopes and fast rigs - practise the Hubble mix and star magic on the kind of data most people actually have.

🔭 The Mono Wing

SHO narrowband channel sets for the mono-curious - combine, balance and palette real filter data without owning a filter wheel.

🐉 The Troublesome Shelf

Famously difficult data - faint dust, stubborn gradients, wild stars. For when you want the practice fight before it happens to your own precious subs.

Why a library? Because the best part of this hobby - turning raw starlight into a picture you’re proud of - shouldn’t have to wait for clear skies, working gear, or a lottery win. Download a master, open it in AstroWizard (or any tool you like), and learn by doing. Every set will be free, forever.

Like everything the wizard builds, the Library will be founded on, and maintained by, kindness.
